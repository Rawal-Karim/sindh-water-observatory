# Data attribution

`structures.geojson` is a derived OpenStreetMap database, copyright OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . Attribution: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Source: Geofabrik Pakistan extract, data through 2026-09-21, https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/pakistan.html . The full derived database is included. The extraction script is `extract-structures.py`.

`sindh.geojson` uses geoBoundaries gbOpen PAK ADM1, public domain. Sentinel-derived imagery contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data. Reference imagery remains hosted by its providers and is subject to their terms; no reference imagery is included in this repository.

The water texture is AI-generated appearance material, not an observation. No Maxar Open Data imagery is included.
